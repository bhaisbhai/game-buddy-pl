Race school

Three laps per race, twenty wild tracks in five cups, including a Mystery Track that's different every time. Drift through corners, launch over the gaps and learn each track's trick. Win a cup by scoring the most points over all four races of a cup. You must finish in the top 4 of each cup race to qualify for the next one; finish 5th or 6th and you race it again. On every track the final lap has a twist, so save something for the end.

Hyper mode
  • Death Race: a race where every kart has three balloons. Hits and falls pop them; lose all three and you're out. First over the line, or last kart standing, wins.
  • Difficulty (title screen): Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert changes how fast and clever the computer racers are in every mode.
  • Lava Lagoon: hit the boost pad to clear the lava bridge and dodge the volcano's fireballs.
  • Monsoon Mumbai: jump the rooftop gaps, and stop for the local train at the level crossing. Wet patches make drifts slide further and charge faster.
  • Himalaya Pass: the frozen lake is slippery, so steer early. On the way down, an avalanche chases the pack to the finish line.
  • Sky Temple: a glider pops open off the big jump. Steer it in the air and fly straight across the U-bend to the far side. Hold brake (L2 / S) to dive and land where you want. Golden sky rings boost you, and crosswinds push you sideways.
  • Manteca, CA: dust devils roam the farm roads and will spin you out, so steer round them. Tumbleweeds knock your speed. Jump the broken overpass over Highway 120.
  • Streatham: it's Bonfire Night. Red buses pull out from the stops, so don't rear-end one. Potholes bounce you, foxes dart out, and the railway cutting needs a run-up.
  • Dino Canyon: a triceratops stampede charges across the first straight (stop or squeeze through), dino dung slows you down and splats your screen, and the stegosaurus is windy. Jump the tar pit.
  • Gorilla Jungle: the giant gorilla on the temple throws coconuts (spin) and poo (splat) at you, and his chest-beats shake the ground. Jump the river.
  • Space Station: low gravity, so jumps go a long way. Drive into a wormhole: usually it's a shortcut, sometimes it sends you back. Keep out of the UFO's green beam, and watch for frozen wee from the runaway space toilet.
  • Kitchen Counter & Bathroom Dash: toy-sized racing. Dodge the flying toast and the cockroach; slide on soap, and keep away from the toilet when it flushes.
  • Aztec Temple: outrun the rolling boulder, time the fire jets, and watch out for the llamas. Sunny Beach: waves wash over the road and the seagulls pinch your items.
  • Santa's Village: drive through Santa's falling presents for free items; dodge snowballs and coal. Pinball Panic: bumpers bounce you and steel balls roll across. Coral Reef: floaty jumps, bubble boosts, jellyfish and an octopus.
  • Whispering Forest: trees come crashing down across the road and the red mushrooms bounce you high. Starship: dodge the laser turrets and get through the blast doors before they slam. Glider Peaks: four glider jumps; ride the updrafts and fly through the golden rings. Mystery Track: a new random track every time.
  • Final lap twist: when the leader starts the last lap, every track turns nasty: the volcano erupts, Mumbai floods, a blizzard and mega avalanche hit the pass, a storm rolls over the Sky Temple, a dust storm blows tumbleweeds across Manteca, and Streatham's fireworks start landing on the road.
  • Battle mode: three balloons each, last one standing wins. Chili and drift boosts let you ram a balloon off. Arenas: Nani's Thali (look out for the spoon), Car Park 2am (stay out of the searchlight), Moon Base (low gravity, meteors), Yeti's Ice Rink (no grip, snowballs), plus two arenas built over three floors: Dream Sequence Studio (red-carpet stairs, a top stage and a wind machine that blows you off ledges) and the Shopping Centre at closing time (escalators, a food court and a floor polisher that leaves slippery wet patches).
  • Drift boosts: hold drift while steering. Sparks go blue, orange, then purple. Let go for a bigger boost each time.
  • Rocket start: press accelerate just as "1" vanishes. Too early and you stall.
  • Water and sky: In Splashdown Park's pools your kart turns into a boat, and its propeller keeps you moving even off the accelerator. In Dogfight Canyon, press the item button while gliding to fire foam darts at the other gliders.
  • Items: Chili (boost), Triple Chili, Goo (drop behind), Bee (hunts the racer ahead), Bubble (blocks one hit), Bouncy Ball (curves towards the kart in front, bounces off walls), Boomerang (flies out and comes back, hits up to two), Rainbow Rush (invincible and faster for 7 seconds; flatten anyone you touch), Duck Flood (giant rubber ducks rain down on the track ahead and bounce everywhere), Slow Motion (rare, and never more than one per race: everyone except you drops to slow motion for 5 seconds), Golden Chili (tap for unlimited boosts for 7 seconds), Triple Goo, Triple Bouncy Balls and Bee Swarm (three shots; while you hold them they block hits), Comet (flies to the leader and crashes down on them), Cherry Bomb (short fuse, big blast), Rocket Ride (autopilot rocket: very fast and invincible), Shrink Ray (everyone else shrinks and can be squashed), Paint Splat (paints the screens of everyone ahead), Ghost Sheet (become a ghost and steal someone's item), Hungry Lunchbox (snaps at karts and items in front of you), Fireballs (throw fireballs for 8 seconds), Party Bag (eight assorted specials), Sunshine Cloud (a happy little cloud makes you drive better for 8 seconds: more grip, faster, steers you away from walls, and clears stink and splats, and it rains gold coins on you the whole time), Spring (battle: jump on someone to steal a balloon), Monster Truck (battle: giant tyres, can't be hit, crushes anyone you touch), Balloon Pump (battle: get a balloon back), Sticky Mine (battle: drop a mine that blows up whoever drives near it) and Freeze Ray (battle: freezes the nearest rival; ram them to pop a balloon) and Coin Bag (+4 coins), Rain Cloud (rains on the leader: slower and slippery), Stinky Cheese (drop behind; green haze and wobbly steering), Jumbo (grow giant and flatten karts), Big Slap (a giant hand slaps everyone right next to you), Belly Flop (a giant hippo flops down ahead: everyone in front who's on the ground slows down and drops their item; hop to dodge), Prank Box (drop a fake item box; its question mark is upside down) and Spare Tyres (two tyres circle your kart for 15 seconds and each one soaks up a hit). You can carry two at once: the big box is the one you fire, the small NEXT box moves up after. Press d-pad ↑ to swap them. Coins raise your top speed.

Death Race

A full race, but everyone carries three balloons. Item hits, boosted rams and falling off the track each pop one. Lose all three and you're out of the race. Cross the line first to win, or be the last kart still standing.

Battle mode

Everyone starts with three balloons. Hit a rival with an item, a boosted ram or Hyper mode to pop one. Lose all three and you're out. The last kart with a balloon wins, or whoever has the most when the 2:30 clock runs out.

Pick an arenaSix arenas, each with its own hazard. The last two are built over several floors. Pop all three of everyone's balloons.

Where to?Five cups of four races, points for every finish. Or pick a single track.
