# Wheelie Fun: itch.io submission kit

Everything needed to publish on itch.io. Go to itch.io → Dashboard → **Create new project**, then fill in each field below.

## Files in this folder

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `wheelie-fun-itch.zip` | The game. Upload under **Uploads** and tick "This file will be played in the browser". |
| `cover_630x500.png` | **Cover image** (itch's recommended size). |
| `screenshots/1_title.png` … `5_phone.png` | **Screenshots** (upload all five, in order). |

## Description (paste into the description box)

> **Race, bash and wheelie your way to the trophy!**
>
> Wheelie Fun is a colourful kart racer you can play right in your browser, on a computer or a phone.
>
> 🏁 **8 cups, 32 tracks.** Beaches, Mumbai monsoons, Himalayan ice, the moon, haunted temples and a bathroom.
> 🐝 **Bonkers items.** Bees, chillies, goo, bouncy balls, duck floods, teleports and more.
> 🏎️ **10 racers.** Each with their own feel, from rocket-start babies to a flying ace.
> 🎨 **My Kart.** Earn coins every race and spend them on paint, helmets, under-glow and monster wheels.
> 🎁 **Daily bonus.** Your first race each day pays double, and streaks earn extra.
> 👥 **Play together.** Up to 4 players on one screen, plus Battle and Death Race modes.
>
> **Controls**
> - Keyboard: ▲ / W to drive, ◀ ▶ / A D to steer, Space to use your item, V to drift
> - Phone: hold ◀ ▶ to steer, tap the round button to use your item
> - Controllers are supported too
>
> Your progress saves automatically in your browser.
>
> Feedback is very welcome: let me know in the comments what you love, what's too hard, and where you got stuck!

## Before you press Public

1. Save as Draft, open the page, click **Run game** and play one race on a computer.
2. Open the same page on your phone and play a race with the new buttons.
3. Switch visibility to **Public**.

## Notes

- The zip holds a single `index.html` with everything inside it (no other files), so nothing can go missing.
- I tested it embedded in a frame on a separate site, the way itch shows it: it loads, saves progress and starts a race with no errors.
- Saves are per browser, so progress on itch is separate from game-buddy.co.uk.
- Online multiplayer still connects to the game-buddy server from itch, so it works there too.
